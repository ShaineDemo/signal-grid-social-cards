# Publish copy system

Every card package also needs a publish-ready Xiaohongshu/RedNote copy package. Write it from the same fact ledger, selected language, and narrative thesis as the images.

## Output format

Create `POST_COPY.md`:

```markdown
# Recommended title
...

## Alternative titles
- ...
- ...
- ...

## Post copy
...

## Suggested hashtags
#... #... #...

## Disclosure / availability note
...
```

## Title rules

- Chinese recommended titles normally fit 14–28 Chinese characters.
- English recommended titles normally fit 5–14 words and should read like native social/editorial copy, not a word-for-word translation.
- Name the concrete subject and strongest change.
- A useful tension or boundary can improve accuracy and curiosity: “能聊天，但暂时不能控车”.
- Do not use “史诗级”, “炸裂”, “颠覆”, or “遥遥领先” without evidence.
- Do not make the title broader than the released feature or supported vehicles.

Provide 2–3 alternatives with distinct angles:

- News angle: what shipped.
- User angle: what changes in use.
- Boundary angle: what it can and cannot do.

## Body structure

For a product/news post, default to 220–500 Chinese characters or 120–240 English words:

1. One-sentence hook with the verified event.
2. Two or three short paragraphs explaining capability, access conditions, and how to try it.
3. One interpretation paragraph explaining why it matters; identify it as interpretation rather than fact.
4. One limitation or availability note.
5. Optional specific question that invites informed discussion.

Use bullets only when they make conditions or features easier to scan. Avoid repeating every card verbatim.

## Platform tone

- RedNote/Xiaohongshu is the default: conversational, concise, useful; use short paragraphs and 3–6 relevant hashtags. English posts should remain readable for an international or bilingual Xiaohongshu audience and avoid unexplained local slang.
- Weibo: more compressed and news-forward; one strong first paragraph and 2–4 hashtags.
- X: follow the four-media and 280-character rules in `platform-system.md`; longer visual stories become threads.
- LinkedIn: lead with the professional implication, add evidence and a measured takeaway, and use 0–3 precise hashtags.
- WeChat: more explanatory; hashtags are optional.

If the platform is unspecified, optimize for Xiaohongshu/RedNote rather than a generic cross-platform compromise.

## Fact discipline

- Use the same source hierarchy as the cards.
- If rollout is staged, say so in the body even when the title is short.
- Distinguish “available”, “announced”, “testing”, and “planned”.
- Do not convert vendor-wide model capabilities into features of the specific released product.
- When reports conflict, use the official current boundary and record the conflict in `SOURCES.md`.
